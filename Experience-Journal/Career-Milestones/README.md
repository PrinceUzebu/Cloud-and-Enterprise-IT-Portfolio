## The Shape Of The Transition

The useful thing about moving from service desk into engineering is that you don't start from zero. You start with a few thousand real diagnostic reps and no architecture vocabulary. The gap I'm actively closing is the second half: design reasoning, change management discipline, and the ability to write something down clearly enough that a security lead can sign off against it.

The [AppLocker project](../Cloud-Projects/AppLocker-Isolation.md) was the first time both halves showed up in the same piece of work. The front-line instinct caught a sensor lag that looked like a broken configuration. The engineering side is what made the policy defensible instead of just functional.
