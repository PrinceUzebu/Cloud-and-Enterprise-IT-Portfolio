## The Shape Of This

I did not come into IT through the front door. I joined Hummingbird in patient access, doing tech-enabled support by phone, email, and chat. Along the way I picked up an Epic analyst contract during an enterprise migration and spent five months as a junior Scrum Master helping the Data and Analytics division move to Agile. None of that is a traditional infrastructure background, and all of it turned out to matter: the patient access work is where the diagnostic instinct came from, the Epic work is why clinical systems aren't foreign, and the Scrum Master stint is why working inside a sprint-based engineering team isn't new.

The actual pivot was **April 2026**, moving onto the Service Desk. Every project in this portfolio has happened since then.

Worth noting the order, though: I earned AI-900 in 2025, before Hummingbird hired me and well before an IT role was on the table. If anyone asks whether the move into infrastructure was opportunistic, that's the date I'd point at.

What has changed in that window is larger than the title. Internal administrator ownership of two enterprise platforms, an end-to-end production security project, Azure administration backed by AZ-104, a root cause analysis that overturned a confident reported diagnosis, and an explicit development track toward infrastructure. The scope arrived ahead of the title, which is the pattern I would rather be able to point at.

My current official role is IT Support Specialist. I am not an Infrastructure Engineer or a Cloud Engineer yet, and this repo will not claim otherwise until the title is real. What it does document is the work being done in the meantime.
