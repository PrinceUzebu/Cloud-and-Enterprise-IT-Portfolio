## The Shape Of This

The through-line isn't a straight climb through job titles. It's scope arriving ahead of the title, which is the pattern I'd rather be able to point at.

The title moved from Service Desk Technician to IT Support Specialist. What actually changed was larger than that: internal administrator ownership of two enterprise platforms, an end-to-end production security project, real Azure administration backed by AZ-104, and an explicit development track toward infrastructure. None of that required a new title to start, and all of it is the argument for the next one.

My current official role is IT Support Specialist. I'm not an Infrastructure Engineer or a Cloud Engineer yet, and this repo won't claim otherwise until the title is real. What it does document is the work being done in the meantime.
