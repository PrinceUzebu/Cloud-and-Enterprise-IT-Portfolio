## About

I'm a Microsoft Certified Azure Administrator (AZ-104) working as an **IT Support Specialist** at Hummingbird Healthcare, a HIPAA-regulated healthcare organization. Day to day that means Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft 365, identity and access, endpoint and enterprise application support, and owning incidents from first troubleshooting through resolution or escalation.

Alongside that role I'm Hummingbird's internal **Monday.com Administrator** and platform point of contact, responsible for the organization's enterprise environment: boards, workflows, automations, permissions, user support, and translating what business teams need into something the platform can actually do. Both Monday.com certifications are complete as of October 2026.

My direction is **Cloud and Infrastructure Engineering**, and I'm building toward it deliberately: Azure, infrastructure as code, automation, and deeper identity work.

This isn't a repo of tutorial labs. It's real production problems, sanitized for confidentiality, written up the way I'd walk a hiring manager through them. Where something is certification knowledge rather than production experience, it says so.

**Where I'm headed:** Service Desk Technician → IT Support Specialist → Cloud/Infrastructure Engineer
