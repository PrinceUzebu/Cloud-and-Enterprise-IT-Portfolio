## 🚀 About This Portfolio

I'm an **IT Support Specialist** at Hummingbird Healthcare, a HIPAA-regulated healthcare organization, working toward Infrastructure and Cloud Engineering. This isn't a repo full of tutorial-following labs. It's a record of real enterprise problems I've solved in production, sanitized for confidentiality, and written up the way I'd walk a hiring manager through them in an interview.

The work splits into two tracks that keep reinforcing each other. On the **cloud and infrastructure** side: Azure administration, Microsoft 365, Entra ID and identity, endpoint security policy, and the kind of root-cause diagnostic work that only comes from production. On the **enterprise platform** side: I'm Hummingbird's internal monday.com IT Administrator, which put me in the seat where I own a platform outright rather than support one from the outside.

Front-line support taught me how systems actually fail. Platform ownership taught me what it costs when a change goes out without someone thinking it through first. Both feed directly into where I'm going.

**Where I'm headed:** Service Desk Technician → IT Support Specialist → Cloud/Infrastructure Engineer
